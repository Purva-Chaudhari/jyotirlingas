## 1. Legends

### Kartikeya and Ganesha
- **Shiva Purana, Rudra Samhita, Kumara Khanda, ch. 19–20**: whoever circles the earth first marries first. Ganesha circumambulates his parents seven times and bows seven times; he marries **Siddhi and Buddhi**, daughters of Prajapati Vishvarupa; his sons are **Kshema and Labha**. https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226140.html (index: https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226120.html)
- **Shiva Purana, Kotirudra Samhita, ch. 15** ("origin of the second Jyotirlinga, Mallikarjuna"): https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/shiva-purana-english/d/doc226516.html
  - vv. 3–5: Kartikeya learns of the wedding from **Narada** and leaves for the **Krauncha** mountain despite his parents' objection.
  - vv. 6–12: Shiva sends sages to bring him back; he refuses.
  - vv. 13–16: The parents go to Krauncha; he moves further away (the translation gives "thirty-six kilometres", i.e. about 3 yojanas).
  - vv. 17–18: "Śiva goes there on Amāvasyā day and Pārvatī on the Full Moon day."
  - v. 19: The linga "born of Mallikārjuna became famous in the three worlds."
  - **This chapter does not name Srisailam** and does not explain the name. "Mallika = Parvati, Arjuna = Shiva" is a popular gloss, and the narration presents it as such.
- **Variant**: Wikipedia says Kartikeya went to "Mount Krauncha in Palani" and that "the place where Shiva and Parvati stayed came to be known as Srisailam". https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mallikarjuna_Temple,_Srisailam . Traditions disagree on where Krauncha is, so the narration just says "the Krauncha mountain".

### Chandravati and the jasmine (the trust's own sthala-purana)
Trust: Chandravati, daughter of King Chandragupta, who ruled a place on the far side of the Krishna, worshipped a linga in a valley where a waterfall floated "lakhs of white jasmine flowers"; this is **Mallika (Mallela) Theertham**. The Chenchu ("Chanchulu") tribe built her a cottage and brought her honey and fruit. Her boon was that the Lord should always wear her jasmine garland, "since then … Mallikarjuna Swamy". The trust gives the source as "the 2nd chapter of Chandravatyupakhyanam of Srisaila Khandam, a part of Skanda Puranam". Mallika Theertham is about 54 km from Srisailam, with a waterfall of about 120–130 ft.
- **Variant**: Wikipedia and others say she worshipped with *mallika* (jasmine) and *arjuna* flowers. Another popular etymology says the deity was worshipped with jasmine, "locally called Mallika in Telugu".

### Bhramaramba
- **Shakti Peetha**: one of the 18 Maha Shakti Peethas, where **Sati's neck** fell (Wikipedia).
- **Devi Bhagavata Purana, Book 10, ch. 13**: Arunasura's boon (that no two- or four-legged creature could kill him); Bhramari's six-legged bees kill him and return to her. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhramari
- Trust: the Srisaila Khanda says Mahakali, Mahalakshmi and Mahasaraswati "originated as one Shakti, i.e. Sri Bhramarambika Devi". A **Sri Chakra "established by … Adi Shankaracharya"** stands in her temple. She is called "Arunasurasamhari, Nadha Swarupini".
- "You can hear bees humming" is a popular devotee claim. It is not in the trust text I read, so the narration says "devotees say".

**Conflict**: one secondary source says Krishnadevaraya "built the Mukhamandapa in 1516", which clashes with Harihara II's 1405 mukha mandapa. The narration credits 1405 to Harihara II and the long pillared halls to Krishnadevaraya.

## 3. Rituals and living culture
Times are from the trust's seva descriptions:
- **Suprabhatam** 03:30; after the Go-puja, **Maha Mangala Harathi** 04:30; Panchamrutha Abhishekam 05:00.
- **Pradoshakala Maha Mangala Harathi** 17:30; Panchamrutha Abhishekam 18:00; Kumkumarchana at the Sri Chakra 18:30.
- **Ekantha (Pavalimpu) Seva**: the trust pages say both "09:30" and "10:30 pm".
- **Nine harathis**: Nandi, Pancha, Hamsa, Saptha, Dwadasa, Naga, Nakshatra, Kumbha, and Eka Harathi ("called Maha Mangala Harathi").
- **Dress for sevas**: "Male sevadars shall wear Pancha with Kanduva and females shall wear Saree with Blouse invariably."
- **Sparsha darshan** (touching the linga) is a ticketed service. The trust has suspended it on peak days, e.g. Sundays, Mondays and Kartika Pournami during Kartika masam (notice for 2021).
- **Sanagala Basavanna**: the Nandi before the shrine (trust).
- **Festivals**: Maha Shivaratri Brahmotsavams; Ugadi Mahotsavam, when Kannada devotees walk 600+ km through the Nallamala with kavadis and saris for Bhramaramba, "their daughter" (Deccan Chronicle: https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/srisailam-sees-massive-kannada-padayatra-for-ugadi-1869579); Kartika masam; Dasara.
- **Devotees**: Akka Mahadevi (12th c.), whose name for Shiva is Chennamallikarjuna and who did penance in the caves here; Hemareddy Mallamma; Shankara (Shivanandalahari composed at Srisailam per Wikipedia https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivananda_Lahari).

## 4. Aarti
- The trust describes **Maha Mangala Harathi** with nine forms of lamp, together with Vedic recitation (Rudram, Suprabhatam). I found no published hymn text for the harathi itself.
- **Lyrics in the JSON** (public domain, labelled as not the documented harathi text): Dwadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram v.2 ("श्रीशैलशृङ्गे…") and Shivanandalahari v.50 ("सन्ध्यारम्भविजृम्भितं … सेवे श्रीगिरिमल्लिकार्जुनमहालिङ्गं शिवालिङ्गितम्"), both traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya. Verify the text against an edition, e.g. https://ambuda.org/texts/shivanandalahari/all .
